![](/img/obit-eckersley.jpg)

Security engineer, digital rights activist, AI safety and policy researcher. Beloved in these communities.

Eckersley is most famous as an advocate/developer at the intersection of tech and legal activism. His work on the [Let's Encrypt](https://letsencrypt.org/) free certificate authority, [HTTPS Everywhere](https://www.eff.org/deeplinks/2012/02/https-everywhere-decentralized-ssl-observatory), and the [SSL Observatory](https://www.eff.org/observatory) made the internet significantly less terrible. [Mizza](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=32700262):

> *Let's Encrypt is something we all came to take for granted very quickly, but lots of us remember when getting an SSL certificate was an expensive and tedious process. Deprecating a billion dollar industry overnight and providing better security for internet users everywhere is a hell of a legacy to leave behind, and I hope one that will be an inspiration for generations to come.*

He identified "[device fingerprinting](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-642-14527-8_1)" as a major privacy hole and spent a long time trying to mitigate it. [His PhD thesis](https://isnot.org/phd.pdf) was a characteristic mix of software engineering, economic theory, and law. We'd call it Web3 these days.

He was the [8th person ever](https://www.givingwhatwecan.org/about-us/members) to take the GWWC pledge. [A few places](https://cltc.berkeley.edu/2016/01/28/24-1pm-peter-eckersley-chief-computer-scientist-electronic-frontier-foundation/) say he was on the board of CEA US at some point.

Around 2017 he went into AI risk. His work bridged AI ethics and AI safety, covering [recidivism prediction](https://pde.is/posts/docs/Report-on-Algorithmic-Risk-Assessment-Tools.pdf), [self-driving cars](https://pde.is/posts/selected/deeplinks-2018-03-some-easy-things-we-could-do-make-all-autonomous-cars-safer-faster/), [security against ML hacking](https://pde.is/posts/selected/deeplinks-2016-08-darpa-cgc-safety-protocol/), and [military AI](https://pde.is/posts/selected/deeplinks-2018-08-how-militaries-should-plan-for-ai/) but also big-brain topics like [impossibility theorems](https://arxiv.org/abs/1901.00064) in social choice and ML, the cybersecurity implications of [emulated minds](https://sciendo.com/article/10.2478/jagi-2013-0011), and the [queer theory](https://dl.acm.org/doi/abs/10.1145/3306618.3314231) of human alignment. At the end, he was running the [AI Objectives Institute](https://ai.objectives.institute/) (notable for mobilising people already concerned about corporate maximisation) and mentoring for [PIBBSS](https://pibbss.ai/). He was on [many](https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.07213) [of the](https://arxiv.org/abs/1802.07228) big "technical AI policy" papers of the last few years. [*The Alignment Problem*](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/50489349-the-alignment-problem)  is dedicated to him, after he convinced his friend Brian Christian of it.

During covid he worked intensely on [private contact tracing](https://github.com/pde/private-contact-tracing/blob/master/Predicted_effectiveness_of_privacy_friendly_mobile_contact_tracing_for_COVID_19.ipynb) [algorithms](https://docs.google.com/document/d/16Kh4_Q_tmyRh0-v452wiul9oQAiTRj8AdZ5vcOJum9Y/edit#). More than [one notable](https://www.facebook.com/cwhite1985/posts/pfbid02yqqrtor2zL2nzYhrqBoBkCHnfJfvn9uSiPvhStkAZREjaEukuezWJ3Uxhua1fsoEl) service could not have been done without him.

He received a cancer diagnosis a week before his death. A minor consolation of dying in the present age is [people telling beautiful stories about you](https://twitter.com/search?q=Peter%20Eckersley) for strangers and for posterity. So you can know that he was [fluent](https://twitter.com/orpheuslummis/status/1565952297129201664) in French, that he [organised](https://twitter.com/metasj/status/1565917968592142337) guerilla meetups, that he was an [avid](https://github.com/pde/bay-bridge-bike-model) cyclist, that he was a [memorable](https://twitter.com/liamosaur/status/1566010749356736512) remedial teacher of programming. [This](https://twitter.com/PeevedPeter) is an Eckersley quotes account. Similarly, I can tell you he was [writing code](https://github.com/pde) until one month before his death. He lived sans LinkedIn.
