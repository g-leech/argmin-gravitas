I'm grateful to you all for being here. I'm grateful that I got 36 years with her - 12% of children get less than this. She always said 'it could be worse'.

I'm grateful we had two months' warning - about a third of people get less than that. I'm grateful that she died at home, the home she made around her over 34 years. I'm grateful for her for never turning on the heating, so that we grew up tough.

She died of toughness, years of thrawn endurance before an unbelievably late diagnosis, when there was 'nothing they could do for her'. Half of cancer victims do as she did, what was done to her: make it to stage 4 before anyone does anything. How different is this from what she would have felt in medieval times? 

Well, regardless, I'm grateful for oxycodone, midazolam, zopiclone, omeprazole, chlorphenamine, levomepromazine, and hyoscine. I give particular thanks to dexamethasone, which let her enjoy her food - so, so much - to the end. 

I am grateful for her grace and patience. Whatever wounds life gave her, she didn't pass it on.

I'm grateful for my brother, who does my head in. I'm grateful to Andrew and Steph for everything. I'm grateful to Jenny for actually paying attention. To Brycie, for tanking the worst, the absolute worst of it. To Billy the nurse, for making her laugh with saucy Doric jokes.

I'm grateful to her for being a tough old bird. And for trying to save everyone's feelings. And for smiling all the time. And for everything. Everything. Everything. And I'm grateful for the jam.
